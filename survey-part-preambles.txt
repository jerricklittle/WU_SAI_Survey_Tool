About SAI surveys

The questions in this section are about Student Assessment of Instruction (SAI) surveys in general, the ones you complete for your courses. They ask whether those surveys meet your needs for giving feedback and are easy to use, how useful and worthwhile you find them, how much effort and attention you give them, and whether you usually answer the open-ended questions and complete an SAI for every class. The last questions ask what you would add, what you would keep, and anything else about the experience of doing SAIs. Answer about SAIs as you normally experience them. These questions are not about the survey you are taking right now.
